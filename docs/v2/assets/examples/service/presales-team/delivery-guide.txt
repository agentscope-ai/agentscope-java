Suggested four-week PoC after prerequisites are met:
1. Confirm scope, access, test data, and acceptance metrics with the customer.
2. Connect knowledge and the read-only order API; keep source and request identifiers.
3. Evaluate correct answers, missing facts, stale order data, permission failures,
   unavailable dependencies, and handoff behavior.
4. Demonstrate, document gaps, and obtain customer acceptance of the PoC.
Do not promise production go-live at the end of the PoC.
Deliverables: proposal.md, requirements.csv, poc-plan.md, open-questions.md.
Every proposed metric needs an agreed target; unspecified targets remain TBD.
Every critical claim cites a document path and version or a real API response.
