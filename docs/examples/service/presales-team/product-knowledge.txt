This is a fictional product catalogue for the Northstar exercise, not a claim
about AgentScope Service's built-in product features.
Supported in the exercise: web portal integration, source-grounded knowledge answers,
read-only order lookup through an authenticated API, and human handoff through a ticket API.
Requires integration: order API authentication, ticket system, and customer identity.
Not offered in this exercise: voice calls or an offline mobile application.
Data residency and SSO support must be confirmed for the target deployment.
Reference: CAT-01
